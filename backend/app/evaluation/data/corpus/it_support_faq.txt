IT SUPPORT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I contact the IT service desk?
The IT service desk is available 24/7 through the ServiceNow portal, by chat in Teams, or by phone at extension 4357.
Priority 1 tickets receive a response within 15 minutes; standard tickets are answered within one business day.

How often are laptops replaced?
Standard laptops are refreshed every three years. Engineering workstations are refreshed every two years.
Damaged devices are replaced immediately after the service desk has assessed the damage.

How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the GlobalProtect client from the Software Center and sign in with your corporate account and MFA.
The VPN gateway address is vpn.northwind.example.

Which collaboration tools are approved?
Microsoft Teams is the approved tool for chat and video meetings. Confluence is used for documentation and Jira for project tracking.
Using personal file-sharing services such as private Dropbox accounts for company data is not allowed.
