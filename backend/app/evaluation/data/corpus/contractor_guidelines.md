# Northwind Contractor Guidelines

These guidelines apply to external contractors and agency staff only. Employees should refer to the Employee Handbook.

## Time Off for Contractors
Contractors do not accrue paid annual leave. Time off must be agreed with the engagement manager and is unpaid.
Contractors engaged for more than six months may take up to 10 unpaid days per year without affecting their contract.

## Contractor Working Hours
Contractors work the hours specified in their statement of work, typically 08:00 to 16:00.
Overtime for contractors is billed at 1.5 times the agreed hourly rate and must be pre-approved in writing.

## Contractor Expenses
Contractor travel is reimbursed only when stated in the statement of work.
Hotel stays for contractors are capped at 150 EUR per night in all cities.
Contractor expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.

## Contractor Equipment
Contractors receive a managed loan laptop, which is replaced only if it is faulty.
Contractor accounts expire automatically at the end of the engagement.
