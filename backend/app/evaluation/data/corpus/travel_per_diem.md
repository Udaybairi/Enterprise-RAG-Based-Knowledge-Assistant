# Travel Per Diem Rates

The following daily rates apply to business travel. Hotel caps are per night; meal per diems are per full travel day.

| City | Hotel cap (EUR/night) | Meal per diem (EUR/day) |
|---|---|---|
| Berlin | 180 | 55 |
| Lisbon | 180 | 45 |
| London | 260 | 75 |
| Zurich | 260 | 90 |
| New York | 260 | 85 |

On travel days that are not full days, 50 percent of the meal per diem is paid.
