# Northwind Employee Handbook

## 1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Core collaboration hours, during which all employees should be reachable, are 10:00 to 15:00.
Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager and is compensated with time off in lieu.

## 2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.
Leave requests must be submitted in the Workday portal at least ten working days in advance.
Up to five unused leave days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

## 3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first year after birth or adoption.

## 4. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week, subject to team agreements.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.
Northwind provides a one-time home office allowance of 750 EUR for ergonomic equipment.

## 5. Expenses
Expense claims must be submitted through Concur within 45 days of the expense date, with itemised receipts.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night in standard cities and 260 EUR per night in high-cost cities such as London, Zurich and New York.
Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours; business class may be booked for longer flights with director approval.
